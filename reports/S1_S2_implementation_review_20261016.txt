Blocking findings:
1. evaluate_cvc_masks.py computes overlap and areas on each model's native output
grid. S2 uses 768 and S0/S1 512. Reproject predictions to the same native-image
grid and evaluate against one unchanged native target. Report negative area as
native pixels and percentage of valid radiograph area. Raw 768 pixel counts are
not directly comparable with 512 counts. Centerline distances use rounded model
coordinates and a nominal scale; recompute on native geometry, explicitly treating
empty predictions as infinite distance and excluding out-of-image path points.

2. Macro Soft Dice in the milestone table is binary thresholded Dice. These are
different from the small soft Dice values used for checkpoint selection.
Select and compare fixed-budget final checkpoints for the primary resolution
experiment; report development-selected best checkpoints separately.

3. Dataset augments image and target with flip/affine but leaves valid_mask fixed.
Apply identical geometric transforms to all three; new affine borders must have
validity zero. Add a non-square-image regression test.

4. train_segmentation_v3.py unconditionally increments successful_updates and steps
the scheduler after GradScaler.step. A skipped optimizer update still gets counted.
Use a real optimizer-step counter and log attempts, skips and scale. Old saved
counts cannot establish 1000 successful updates without additional evidence.

5. Batch-2/accum-2 and batch-1/accum-4 do NOT optimize identical Dice objectives.
Positive-only Dice is normalized by the number of positives in each microbatch.
Example effective batch [P,P,P,N]: batch-2 yields .5*mean(D1,D2)+.5*D3, whereas
batch-1 yields .25*(D1+D2+D3). Normalize summed positive losses by the effective
batch positive count, not each microbatch's count. BCE pixel denominators also
need consistent effective-batch weighting. Verify gradients on a deterministic
toy model. Frozen BN and GroupNorm do not fix loss-normalization differences.

6. Trainer does not seed Torch/augmentation/decoder initialization or persist full
RNG, optimizer, scheduler, scaler, config and input hashes. Only sampler has its
own fixed NumPy seed. Therefore S2 is not a clean resolution-only causal test.
Store initial-state hash and ordered study/augmentation ledger for paired runs.

7. Evaluator cache keys omit resolution/preprocessing/image hash and do not verify
cache shape. Include those plus code/normalization version. No-polyline images
are evaluated as negatives without asserting membership in confirmed negatives;
fail fast on unknown images. Full-image AP is advertised/imported but not computed.
Audit panel actually contains 8 negatives, not 10, and is overwritten by each
evaluation. Freeze its IDs and save model-specific measurements separately.

8. Low missed-instance rate means at least 30% of a path falls within tolerance
of any foreground. It does not mean 244 complete catheters were segmented or
identified individually. Multi-CVC Dice also cannot establish instance recall.
At S2 threshold .70, pixel recall is .3596, centerline coverage .4989 and missed
rate .2258. The high precision at that threshold comes with substantial misses.
Do not mix threshold-.30 recovery and threshold-.70 precision as one result.
S0 JSON missed_rate_10px at .30 is .0323, not .056 in the milestone table.

9. Classifier actual run used 4000 train images and 1000 selection images, not
5000 training images. Later full selection evaluation used 4562 images. Pilot
selection uses df.iloc[:max_samples], not stratified random sampling. Saved
training loss .616 -> .427 while selection AUC stayed about .51-.54 warrants
generalization/optimization diagnosis, not an assumption that more data and
weighting will achieve >90% sensitivity at acceptable specificity.
Evaluate train and selection subsets in eval mode; verify image-label joins,
pretraining, class order, probability variance and BN train/eval behavior. Then
run a tiny fitting test only as a separately authorized diagnostic training run.
Classifier triage currently calls every non-alert acceptable; normal probability
and uncertainty_band are unused. Add explicit normal-evidence and uncertainty/
no-CVC handling before describing outputs as placement decisions.

10. Lower AUC on historically exposed cases cannot prove absence of leakage.
Check patient, duplicate-group, image-hash and upstream fitting/selection exposure
directly. Keep model_selection as development. 8853 annotated studies is the entire
inventory, not an eligible training count; full fitting must intersect approved
training groups and exclude all selection/calibration/test groups.
All-zero CVC labels support CVC-negative supervision; absence of all listed device
labels does not establish the absence of ECG wires or every possible distractor.

2. WHAT THE INSPECTION ESTABLISHED
Files: src/models/coarse.py, coarse_dual.py, losses.py; src/data/datasets.py,
rasterize.py, transforms.py; scripts/05_train_coarse_dual.py; resolved run config.

A. Architecture is a custom, single-channel, base-32 U-Net with three pooling
operations (deepest stride 8), PPM bottleneck, transposed-convolution upsampling,
BatchNorm and shared segmentation/tip decoder. This is NOT the previous refiner's
EfficientNet architecture. It has spatial skip connections and global context;
claiming it lacks all context or all fine-resolution outputs would be incorrect.
The backbone initialization came from our coarse checkpoint, not ImageNet.

B. All native radiographs are reduced to 512x512 letterboxed inputs. A 10-native-
pixel target stroke becomes about 2.05 pixels when a 2500-pixel side maps to 512.
Real catheter contrast and thin paths may be lost. The model cannot reconstruct
image information discarded by preprocessing simply by upsampling its output.

C. Targets are fixed-width rasterized polylines, not manually traced silhouettes.
They support catheter path/corridor learning, not proof of exact physical borders.
Native fixed stroke width also varies in effective model-grid thickness by image
size. Nearest-neighbor reduction can introduce aliasing/discontinuities.

D. Dataset safeguards reject wholly unannotated positives as empty masks. However,
the presence of one polyline does not prove every CVC in that image is annotated.
The current full-image binary loss treats everything outside supplied paths as
background, potentially penalizing unlisted catheters. This is a residual risk,
not a measured prevalence estimate.

E. DiceLoss flattens the entire batch into one Dice calculation. Large foreground
samples influence the batch statistic more than short/sparse paths. Compare with
per-positive-image Dice; retain explicit negative-image BCE supervision.

F. Segmentation uses BCE positive weight 50 and Dice weight 2. The tip head uses
weight 2 and positive weight 100. These objectives share all backbone features.
High positive weighting encourages recall, but is not a complete explanation for
false masks. Earlier weight-only pilots did not solve discrimination. Shared-task
interference is a hypothesis, not a proven root cause.

G. The inspected dataset applies CLAHE, resize and /255 scaling, with no stochastic
augmentation in its loading path. The run has only 1000 successful updates on
796 studies (400 positive,396 negative); it is a pilot, not full-data training.
BatchNorm with small batches is a potential stability issue, not a proven defect.
CLAHE can enhance both catheters and distracting structures; test rather than assume.

H. Semantic union masks do not assign pixels to individual catheters at crossings.
Skeleton endpoints are not automatically distal tips. Largest-component filtering,
nearest-carina rules and narrow anatomical gates can discard real abnormal CVCs.
Global boxes around diffuse foreground cannot produce useful fine-stage ROIs.

I. Reported final validation mask Dice in expanded history is 0.13866 (aggregate
definition must be retained). Tip success is 1/25 provisional reviewed targets;
this is NOT segmentation accuracy. The viewer uses mask threshold 0.30. Earlier
weight-50 precision/recall numbers belong to a different checkpoint and threshold.
Re-evaluate masks directly before ranking future segmentation models.

J. Code/config drift remains: pilot.yaml names greedy matching while the resolved
run uses Hungarian matching; loss defaults differ from effective run weights.
Use one resolved configuration and canonical evaluator, hash both, and store ordered
sample IDs. Seed equality alone does not establish ordered exposure equality.
Existing fixes to geometry and FP32 reductions should be retained. Current shaft
penalty code already uses a negative-only softplus term, but is irrelevant to the
first segmentation-only experiment.

3. DATA CONTRACT — EXISTING DATA CAN SERVE TWO DIFFERENT TASKS
Verified inventory: 11,629 CVC polylines in 8,853 studies (before split/exclusion).
Study inventory: 29,333 CVC-present studies; 726 flagged confirmed negatives.
The 750 all-zero-CVC-label raw studies versus 726 inventory negatives must be
reconciled, not silently conflated. Do not assume another-device-present means CVC absent.

Segmentation eligibility:
- Valid CVC paths: annotated foreground. Record completeness separately.
- Supported CVC-absent study: empty target, background supervision allowed.
- CVC present but no paths: no segmentation loss; usable for classification.
- Known incomplete annotations: exclude from full-background segmentation loss
  initially, or use an explicitly tested partial-label scheme in a later arm.
- Truncated CVC: keep its visible path. Unknown distal identity is irrelevant here.
- Invalid record: quarantine that record/affected task, not automatically every
  other usable label for the image.
- Non-CVC polylines can support distractor analysis. Do not erase overlap pixels
  or assume their annotation inventory is exhaustive.

Classification eligibility:
- Use the three original CVC Normal/Borderline/Abnormal columns on all valid images,
  including images with no segmentation annotations.
- Multi-CVC images may have multiple positive labels. Preserve multilabel targets.
- Contradictory/missing labels need explicit audit flags; never fill unknowns with 0.

Create versioned manifests with study, patient, duplicate connected-group, split,
image path/hash/dimensions, three labels, annotation instance IDs, annotation
completeness status, segmentation validity, and historical fitting/selection exposure.
Preserve existing development comparisons. Fit/tune/calibrate on separated patient-
and-duplicate groups. Reserve an additional internal calibration partition where
feasible, with its historical exposure clearly disclosed. No re-split converts old
exposed images into an untouched project test. External validation remains necessary.

5. SEGMENTATION MODEL S1 — PRIORITY EXPERIMENT
New name: CVC-Segmentation-V3. Output: union probability mask for all visible CVCs.
Architecture proposal: ImageNet-pretrained EfficientNet-B0 encoder plus lightweight
U-Net/FPN-style top-down decoder with lateral fusion at strides 4,8,16,32 and a
stride-2 detail stage; final native-compatible output at input resolution. Keep
decoder widths small (e.g.128,64,32,16). This is a new baseline, not a paper replica.
Do not include tip, anatomy or classification heads in this first training objective.
Use grayscale replicated to three channels and encoder-specific normalization.
Start 512 inputs for memory/optimization checks; compare 768 in the next controlled
arm only if thin-line diagnostics support it. Preserve aspect ratio.
Freeze pretrained encoder BatchNorm running statistics for tiny batches; use GroupNorm
in new decoder blocks. Document the policy; accumulation does not emulate large-batch BN.

Supervision:
Start with the existing fixed-width path target as a transparent compatibility
baseline. Exclude padding. Per-image foreground Dice on positive images plus BCE
over valid pixels on both positives and negatives. Select BCE positive weighting
from a small predeclared development sweep; do not assume 1 or 50 is optimal.
Change weighting only in an isolated follow-up, not alongside resolution/sampling.
Next target experiment, if aliasing is measured: construct antialiased/soft path
targets with documented native-to-model width conversion. Keep evaluation masks
unchanged so target thickening cannot manufacture an improvement.
Exact silhouette claims require a small boundary-annotated benchmark; existing
polylines alone cannot certify catheter wall accuracy.

Sampling and augmentation:
Start with documented 3 positive:1 confirmed negative study sampling, ensuring
multi-CVC and abnormal cases remain represented. Sample patients to limit repeated
study dominance; disclose the altered prevalence. Keep batch composition identical
in paired model arms. Modest rotation/translation/scale and mild brightness/contrast
with joint mask transforms; no vertical flips or aggressive cropping of catheters.
Compare CLAHE on/off later, not as an unlogged preprocessing change.

Hardware:
One GPU training process. Profile batch 2 at 512 then batch 1-2 at 768. Accumulate
gradients to effective batch 4 when needed. Use AMP, GradScaler, FP32 loss reductions,
unscale before gradient clipping, and log real optimizer updates plus AMP skips.
AdamW initial proposal lr1e-4, weight_decay1e-4. Record updates, examples and wall time.
Cache resized images/targets on disk with fingerprints; avoid repeated full-native
rasterization/hashing every epoch. Tune workers 0/2 experimentally on Windows.
Do not treat empty_cache or allocator flags as guarantees against OOM.

6. EXPERIMENT ORDER AND STOP RULES
S0: Frozen mask evaluation; no fitting.
S1: Segmentation-only pretrained baseline, fixed eligible pilot cohort, 1000 updates.
S2: Same model/data/loss, higher resolution, matched successful-update/exposure budget;
    additionally report wall time so higher compute is visible.
S3: Only if false positives persist, training-only hard-negative patch sampling from
    confirmed-negative images. Preserve whole-image positives and negative variety.
    Never mine new training examples from validation/test errors.
S4: Only if correct tracks remain disconnected, small soft-clDice addition, with
    FP-area and false-bridge checks. Connectivity loss can reinforce wrong connections.
Do not run every option by default. Stop to inspect labels/targets if S1 cannot fit
a fixed small positive+negative training set or improves training but not development.

Preflight: geometry roundtrip, visible paths surviving resizing, joint augmentation,
padding ignore, multi-CVC targets, negative/unknown masks, AMP finite backward,
save/reload inference parity and tiny-set fitting. Never use overfit success as
proof of clinical truth or generalization.

Advancement: choose a predeclared operating point by precision/recall tradeoff;
compare precision at matched centerline recall and negative false area. Require
improvement on the fixed varied panel without a multi-CVC recall collapse. Use
patient-bootstrap intervals and repeat the leading comparison across 3 seeds.
Only then expand to all eligible training paths and supported negatives. Full
training length is chosen from learning curves/early stopping, not an accuracy promise.

7. DIRECT PLACEMENT CLASSIFIER — INDEPENDENT TRACK
Name: CVC-Placement-V1. Input: entire radiograph, output three independent sigmoid
scores for CVC Normal, Borderline, Abnormal. Initial encoder: pretrained
EfficientNet-B0, 512 letterbox, global pooling, dropout and linear three-output head.
Use existing image labels; no tip labels or predicted masks are required.
Retain full image to avoid removing misplaced devices. Separate model parameters
from segmentation so failures are measurable and objectives cannot interfere.

Start unweighted multilabel BCE with a documented natural-prevalence sampler.
Evaluate per-label imbalance first; compare weighting/balancing only if needed.
Use 4-6k grouped training studies for the optimization pilot (3 epochs), but a fixed
representative development set, including all available eligible abnormal subgroups.
Then use all eligible classifier training studies with early stopping. Preprocessing,
memory profiling, AMP safeguards and provenance are the same discipline as S1.

Output policy, tuned on separate calibration data:
- Any high-confidence abnormal: needs review.
- Borderline: needs review, retain borderline explanation.
- Normal only with low abnormal/borderline probability: predicted normal placement.
- Low/conflicting confidence or out-of-scope image: uncertain.
- No convincing CVC evidence: no reliable CVC assessment; do not call placement normal.
Study-level labels do not identify which catheter is abnormal. Do not report a
per-catheter placement decision until instance-specific evidence is available.

Metrics: per-label ROC-AUC and AP, abnormal and borderline sensitivity, abnormal
cases incorrectly called normal (false reassurance), specificity on normal-only
studies, positive predictive value, calibration/Brier score and coverage versus
error under abstention. Count referrals separately from correct classifications;
referring everyone cannot be called high placement accuracy. Patient-level intervals,
multi-CVC strata, actual class prevalence and learning curves required.
Image-level overall accuracy alone is unacceptable. No 95% guarantee.

8. COMBINE ONLY AFTER BOTH TRACKS ARE MEASURABLY USEFUL
Serve the original image plus predicted mask and independent placement scores.
Keep an overlay-off option; distinguish probability masks from binary thresholds.
Never erase the full image or force the classifier to view only a predicted mask.
If testing mask-assisted classification, compare against the image-only baseline;
train using out-of-fold predicted masks (not ideal GT masks), and use a frozen
segmentation producer for validation. Preserve producer fitting/selection provenance.
Mask-assistance is optional, not a prerequisite for placement decisions.
Add instance association, verified tip labels and anatomy only in later separate
milestones. No carina/CAJ or physical-millimetre outputs without relevant labels
and image calibration. Current semantic masks are not individual instance masks.
